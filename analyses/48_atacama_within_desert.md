# 48. Atacama within-desert comparison — CSP1-2 location

**Question.** Does Atacama exhibit a CSP1-2 dominance pattern similar to EQ? Where within Atacama is CSP1-2 found?

**Method.** Re-process Atacama 16S data (public); within-Atacama PCoA + logistic for CSP1-2 presence vs environmental covariates. `scripts/run_atacama_within_desert.py`, output `cache/atacama_within_desert_logit.tsv`, `cache/atacama_within_desert_summary.txt`.

**Key results.**
- CSP1-2 is detected in Atacama **but is restricted to the Altiplano (high-altitude wet-margin) sites**.
- Absent from the central hyperarid core.
- Within-Atacama logit: CSP1-2 presence positively associated with elevation + cooler temperature + higher P.

**Interpretation.** CSP1-2 is **not a hyperarid-core taxon** universally — even in Atacama it's a Altiplano-fringe lineage. Strengthens the case that the EQ CSP1-2 "dominance" is **not** a generic hyperarid signature but a context-specific outcome.

**Status.** solid.
