Remaining author-source verification: physical well-to-well analysis

The current manuscript reports 259 Trip 1 biological samples, a mean
Bray-Curtis difference of +0.005 and one-sided p=0.87 from 999 stratified
permutations for physically adjacent wells (shared edge or corner).

The physical plate map, executed script and result output for this specific
analysis have not been found in the ecology/data repositories or other local
project copies. It first appears in Rund's 28 September Overleaf revision fb00f5c and is
reworded in her 2 October revision a5d5623. The older
library-order adjacency analysis is a different test and cannot substantiate
this claim. It has been rerun on corrected coordinates separately.

Rund's well-to-well wording and numbers are retained pending her source files;
they have not been independently verified or rerun. At least the Trip 1 Site 52
coordinate must be checked if that sample is included in the plate-map cohort.
This prevents an unconditional claim that every manuscript analysis is fully
verified. Needed: sample-to-plate/well map, analysis code, result output and
random seed/permutation specification. Package those inputs, reproduce the
reported result and rerun with corrected coordinates before submission.

A targeted read-only search of the shared IBEX EmptyQuarter_Data soil/amplicon
project, for-rund and emptyquarter directories also found no physical well map
or adjacency script; available Trip 1 CSVs contain biological/site metadata only.
