# 37. Nibribacter — XRF chemistry & climate substitution

**Question.** What soil chemistry & climate niche does Nibribacter occupy?

**Method.** Per-(site, comp), regress Nibribacter relative abundance against XRF elements and climate variables. `scripts/nibribacter_xrf_substitution.py`.

**Key results.**
- **Prefers sandy, non-saline sites**: negatively associated with SO₃, Na, Cl, Ca, Mg (sabkha proxies); positively with Si.
- Climate: modest positive correlation with d7 precip — pulse-responsive.
- Distinct from the halotolerant Bacilli (Strategy B), which prefer the opposite chemistry.

**Interpretation.** Nibribacter occupies the **anti-sabkha niche** — sandy DOM-rich soils. Pairs with Strategy A (#39) on the sandy side of the XRF axis (#2).

**Status.** solid.
